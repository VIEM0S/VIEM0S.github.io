CultureCase is an iPhone case brand with Malian designs. With hundreds of model × design combinations, knowing what was left in stock quickly became the real problem. The tool grew step by step.

How it's built

Technical choices

Before May 2026, I lost data. Every choice below comes from that: a management tool that loses sales is useless.

  1. 01

    Never lose data again

    Data lives in Firestore, synced in real time across devices. Every change is also copied locally, and a full backup goes to Google Sheets every week (the last eight are kept). No data has been lost since.

  2. 02

    Selling without a connection

    CultureCase GS installs like an app (PWA) and keeps working offline. Operations sync when the network returns, and an indicator shows the sync status.

  3. 03

    A single source of truth

    The online store reads the catalogue, prices and stock directly from the back office. It can only create pending orders and reviews, which I approve. Designs are recognised even after being renamed.

  4. 04

    Security and privacy

    Firestore security rules separate the admin, a read-only viewer mode and the public. Contact details from orders that were never processed are deleted automatically after a while.

  5. 05

    Built to last

    91 automated tests cover inventory, sales, orders and backup logic. Every update is tested and deployed automatically (GitHub Actions), and errors hit by the app are logged so they can be fixed.
